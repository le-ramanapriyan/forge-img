A few useful answers

Before you begin.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Processing happens on your device, inside your browser. Your files never leave the browser.

Does compression change image dimensions?

No. Compress keeps the original width and height. Use Resize when you want to change dimensions.

How does cropping a batch work?

The preview uses the first image. The same crop selection is applied to the whole batch.

What does Lottie fallback export?

The first frame as WebP, JPEG, or PNG, at the original composition width and height. Linked images are picked up automatically.
